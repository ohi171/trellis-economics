Why Trellis

Research-grade methods, built for real decisions.

Our work begins with the policy problem—not a preferred model. We match the question to the data, make assumptions visible, and build outputs that technical and nontechnical audiences can use.

  • Methodological rigorCausal inference, econometrics, machine learning, and sensitivity analysis.
  • Data depthHousehold surveys, longitudinal data, administrative records, international databases, and geospatial sources.
  • Reproducible deliveryDocumented workflows, quality checks, clear code, and transparent analytical decisions.
  • Policy translationTechnical reports, policy briefs, visualizations, and public-facing writing.

How we work

A clear path from question to use.

Engagements are scoped around the decision, available evidence, timeline, and audience. Every stage is documented and reviewable.

Define the decision, research question, and intended use.

Assess

Design

Analyze

Build reproducible analysis and test key assumptions.

Translate

Deliver technical and policy-facing outputs for action.
