Services

Research support from design to decision.

We help clients frame policy questions, assess data, choose appropriate methods, produce reliable analysis, and communicate what the evidence means.

01

Impact evaluation and causal research

We design and implement empirical studies that identify policy effects while making assumptions and limitations explicit.

  • Difference-in-differences and event studies
  • Instrumental variables and panel-data models
  • Policy and institutional change analysis
  • Robustness, sensitivity, and heterogeneity analysis
  • Statistical analysis plans and reproducible code

02

Health and human development analytics

We analyze inequalities in health and human development using household surveys, administrative sources, and international databases.

  • Maternal and child health
  • Healthcare access and utilization
  • Poverty, nutrition, and household vulnerability
  • Gender-disaggregated analysis
  • Cross-country indicators and reporting

03

Data systems and indicators

We turn fragmented data into reliable analytical assets supported by documentation and quality assurance.

  • Data cleaning, harmonization, and integration
  • Indicator construction and validation
  • Cross-country and longitudinal databases
  • Data dictionaries and technical documentation
  • Reproducible workflows and quality checks

04

Climate and geospatial analytics

We link environmental exposures with socioeconomic and health data to study vulnerability, impacts, and adaptation.

  • Temperature, disaster, and water-quality exposure
  • Survey-to-spatial-data linkage
  • Geographic inequality and vulnerability profiles
  • Geospatial quality assurance
  • Maps and policy-facing visualizations

05

Predictive analytics and machine learning

We use predictive methods when the goal is classification, targeting, or model comparison—and distinguish prediction clearly from causal inference.

  • LASSO and regularized regression
  • Random forest and gradient boosting
  • Cross-validation and performance assessment
  • Risk and service-access classification
  • Interpretation for policy applications

06

Research and grant partnerships

We strengthen research programs and funding applications through focused questions, credible methods, and realistic analytical plans.

  • Evidence-gap and feasibility assessment
  • Concept notes and technical narratives
  • Methods, workplans, and dissemination plans
  • Consortium and subcontracting support
  • Peer-review and revision support

07

Evidence communication

We translate technical analysis into outputs suited to decision-makers, practitioners, researchers, and public audiences.

  • Technical reports and working papers
  • Policy briefs and evidence summaries
  • Data visualizations and presentations
  • Public-facing research articles
  • Stakeholder-ready findings decks

08

Training and analytical capacity

We develop practical, project-centered training for research teams working with applied economic and development data.

  • Stata, R, and Python workflows
  • Applied econometrics and evaluation methods
  • Survey-data and indicator analysis
  • Reproducible research practices
  • Interpretation and communication of results

Ways to work together

Flexible support for different stages of a project.

Engagements can be short diagnostics, defined analytical assignments, or longer research partnerships.

Rapid evidence and data diagnostic

A focused assessment of the question, available data, analytical options, risks, and next steps.

Defined research project

A scoped study covering design, analysis, documentation, reporting, and dissemination.

Embedded analytical support

Flexible research and data capacity integrated into an organization's existing team.

Evaluation partnership

Analytical leadership or support for the design and implementation of a program evaluation.

Research consortium

Economics, data, and evaluation expertise for grant-funded or multi-institution partnerships.

Training engagement

Tailored workshops built around the team's data, methods, and decision context.

For mixed-method assignments, we collaborate with qualitative specialists and field partners whose experience fits the research context.
