Research built around consequential policy questions.

Environmental healthEducation

Water quality and children's education in India

Examined how water quality affects children's reading and writing outcomes across districts in the Ganges Basin.

Data
Indian Human Development Survey and water-quality monitoring data
Methods
District-level exposure linkage and applied econometric analysis
Output
Peer-reviewed article in Environment and Development Economics

Working paper
